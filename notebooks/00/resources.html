Resources#

Books#

  • Hamilton, J. D. (1994). Time Series Analysis. Princeton University Press.

  • Canova, F. (2007). Methods for Applied Macroeconomic Research. Princeton University Press.

  • Kilian, L., and Lütkepohl, H. (2017). Structural Vector Autoregressive Analysis. Cambridge University Press.

  • Burns, A. F., and Mitchell, W. C. (1946). Measuring Business Cycles. National Bureau of Economic Research. The source of the 6–32 quarter business-cycle band (chapter 6).

  • Bry, G., and Boschan, C. (1971). Cyclical Analysis of Time Series: Selected Procedures and Computer Programs. National Bureau of Economic Research. The monthly dating rules that BBQ adapts to quarterly data (chapter 8).

  • Dagum, E. B., and Cholette, P. A. (2006). Benchmarking, Temporal Distribution, and Reconciliation Methods for Time Series. Lecture Notes in Statistics 186, Springer. A reference treatment of Denton and regression-based disaggregation (chapter 4).

  • Bowley, A. L. (1901). Elements of Statistics. P. S. King & Son. The quartile skewness coefficient (chapter 8).

  • Kendall, M. G., and Stuart, A. The Advanced Theory of Statistics, Vol. 1. Charles Griffin (several editions). The mean-minus-median skewness measure (chapter 8).

Software and data#

  • econts and this book’s source: github.com/hmorao95/econ-time-series-manual. econts.load reads the bundled FRED snapshot or fetches any FRED series.

  • FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis: fred.stlouisfed.org. The source of every bundled series.

  • pandas: pandas.pydata.org. McKinney, W. (2010). Data structures for statistical computing in Python. Proceedings of the 9th Python in Science Conference, 56–61. doi:10.25080/Majora-92bf1922-00a

  • statsmodels: statsmodels.org. Seabold, S., and Perktold, J. (2010). Statsmodels: econometric and statistical modeling with Python. Proceedings of the 9th Python in Science Conference, 92–96. doi:10.25080/Majora-92bf1922-011

  • X-13ARIMA-SEATS, US Census Bureau: census.gov/data/software/x13as.html. The x13as executable that statsmodels.tsa.x13 calls.

  • R seasonal, the most convenient X-13 interface in R: Sax, C., and Eddelbuettel, D. (2018). Seasonal adjustment by X-13ARIMA-SEATS in R. Journal of Statistical Software, 87(11). doi:10.18637/jss.v087.i11

  • JDemetra+, the seasonal adjustment software recommended by Eurostat and the ECB: github.com/jdemetra

  • R tempdisagg, the reference implementation of Denton, Chow–Lin, Fernández and Litterman: cran.r-project.org/package=tempdisagg. Sax, C., and Steiner, P. (2013). Temporal disaggregation of time series. The R Journal, 5(2), 80–87. doi:10.32614/RJ-2013-028

  • Empirical Macro Toolbox (MATLAB), by Filippo Ferroni: naffe15.github.io/EMT. Several recipes in chapters 2, 3, 6, 7 and 8 port its functions; its function map lists the originals.

  • housestyle, the figure package of chapter 11: github.com/hmorao95/housestyle

  • Time Series Analysis with Python, by Filippo Maria Bianchi: github.com/FilippoMB/python-time-series-handbook. The model for this book’s structure (summaries, exercises and a knowledge test).
